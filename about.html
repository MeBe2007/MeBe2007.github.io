The Dandelion Collective Botanica / Boomtown 2026

About the Project

This creative project was led by The Dandelion Collective — a group of 7 young people from Rushmoor aged 18 and 19 — as part of the Artswork Young Cultural Changemakers programme, in collaboration with Army Welfare Services and Boomtown Festival. The young cultural changemakers chose to explore themes of interconnection, community, and environmental issues through a project focus on mycelium.

After a call out for professional artists to work on the project, a shortlist of artists was selected to pitch to The Dandelion Collective for the chance to be lead artist on the project commission. The young people chose to work with two of the artists who pitched, selecting Abigail Smith as lead artist and Zoe Gibbons as textiles specialist/supporting artist.

After the initial artist selection and brainstorming sessions, many of the young people involved as part of The Dandelion Collective were busy with university exams until mid-July. To widen participation from local young people, Artswork and the Army Welfare Services invited four local youth groups to contribute creative elements to the project, with direction and (where possible) participation from members of The Dandelion Collective.

In the last week of July, The Dandelion Collective put together the installation in its current form, incorporating the various creative outputs of the youth group sessions into the final piece. If you look closely, you might be able to spot a few!

  • Can you find the Mossy Hoops done by our Youth Sewing Group? (Hint: Look a little closer at the tree stump!)
  • Which is your favourite of the mini-mushrooms made by local youth groups QP and SYG?
  • Follow the glittering snail trails to spot the psychedelic snails made by local young people!
