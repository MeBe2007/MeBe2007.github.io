The Terrarium

The Dandelion Collective Botanica / Boomtown 2026

WELCOME TO THE TERRARIUM

Step inside a living digital ecosystem created to celebrate community, interconnection, and environmental action.

Inspired by the unseen networks of mycelium that connect forest floors beneath our feet, this installation brings together the creative vision of local young people, artists, and youth groups across Rushmoor. Like the fungal threads that sustain whole forests, every element here was built through collaboration and shared roots.

Use the menu icon above to explore the story behind the project, meet the artists and young changemakers who built it, and discover hidden community artwork woven into the piece!
